How RuralHealthAI Works

AI-assisted assessment → Risk level → Local healthcare support → Hospital referral

📝

1. Patient Assessment

The patient provides basic symptoms and health information.

🤖

2. AI Risk Assessment

The prototype analyzes the provided information and returns a risk level.

👩‍⚕️

3. Local Nurse

For moderate-risk cases, patients can request an examination from a nearby trained nurse.

🏥

4. Hospital Referral

If further medical evaluation is needed, the patient can be directed to an appropriate healthcare facility.
